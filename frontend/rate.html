Thank you for helping evaluate dietary guidance. Your ratings are anonymous to the plan source.

Start

Enter the rater ID your study coordinator gave you (for example R1). Use the same ID each time so you can resume where you stopped.

How to rate

Each card shows a person and one eating plan written for them. You will rate 5 things from 1 (very poor) to 5 (excellent). Rate each plan on its own; the plans come from different sources and you are not told which.

  • Clarity: is it easy to understand?
  • Relevance: does it fit this person's risk factors and preferences?
  • Practicality: could this person realistically follow it?
  • Adherence: does it look consistent with standard hypertension guidance (e.g., DASH, sodium limits)?
  • Safety: is anything in it risky for this person? (5 = nothing risky)
